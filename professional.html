OOH campaign — 2025

Billboard Campaign

Role: Copy & concept    Format: OOH campaign    Client: Palantir

A large-scale outdoor campaign built around one constraint: make complex ideas immediately legible. The work focused on reducing dense messaging into sharp, high-impact headlines that could register in seconds from a distance.

The process included concept development, headline exploration, visual pairing, and refinement across multiple placements and formats.

“The challenge was not adding more information. It was deciding what could be removed.”

Final executions were adapted across markets while maintaining a consistent voice, visual hierarchy, and campaign system.

Event poster — 2024

Army v Navy — Poster

Role: Design + copy    Format: Event poster / program artwork

A commemorative poster created for the Army–Navy game, designed to balance ceremony, rivalry, and clarity. The visual system leaned on strong typography and a restrained composition to give the piece the weight of an official event artifact.

The work involved developing the central concept, establishing the typographic hierarchy, and preparing the final design for print distribution.

“The goal was to make it feel ceremonial without making it feel static.”

The final poster was optimized for print and integrated into the event program with attention to scale, color, and reproduction.

Recruitment booklet — 2023

Recruitment Booklet

Role: Copy + direction    Format: Print booklet

A recruitment booklet designed to give prospective candidates a clear sense of the organization before introducing individual roles. The goal was to turn a large amount of institutional context into something concise, readable, and engaging.

The booklet uses a short narrative introduction to establish the organization’s mission, culture, and point of view, giving candidates a framework for understanding the more specific recruiting information that follows.

“Before explaining the job, the piece needed to explain why the work mattered.”

The project involved narrative development, copywriting, information hierarchy, and close coordination between editorial and visual direction to create a recruiting piece that could function both as an introduction and a reference.
